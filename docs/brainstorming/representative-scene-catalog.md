# Wilson Shipwrecked — Representative Scene Catalog

> Archived brainstorming artifact produced by an independent game/systemic narrative design pass.
>
> **Purpose:** preserve the original representative scene catalog as a player-visible behavioral reference and source material for scene rendering/regression tests.
>
> **Status:** brainstorming/test inspiration, not a canonical feature or architecture specification. Canonical triage and system implications live in `../SCENE_VALIDATION.md`.

The original catalog contains 40 representative scenes, including ordinary autonomous life, physical experimentation, player intervention, persistent object/animal history, projects, weather, rare/directed events, danger/death/resurrection, and long-history payoffs.

## Catalog source

The preserved source artifact is **Wilson Shipwrecked — Representative Scene Catalog**, produced independently from the product brief. It should be treated as the original scene-design output: scene setups, visible sequences, variations, player opportunities, persistent consequences, design stress tests, independent analysis, product tensions, and missing-scene categories.

## Rendering-test usage

When deriving scene rendering tests from this catalog:

- preserve the player-visible beats rather than hidden-system explanations;
- tests may isolate a scene and directly construct its prerequisite state;
- a rendering test does not imply the final simulation must script the scene beat-for-beat;
- prefer scenes that exercise reusable poses, interaction anchors, camera readability, prop relationships, environmental staging, and emotional/body-language readability;
- keep canonical behavioral/system requirements in `BEHAVIORAL_MODEL.md`, `SCENE_VALIDATION.md`, and `ARCHITECTURE.md` rather than promoting brainstorming details into requirements automatically.
